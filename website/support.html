Managing your subscription

Subscriptions are billed and managed through your Apple ID. To view, change, or cancel a subscription, open the Settings app on your iPhone → tap your name → Subscriptions.

Common questions

  • Is my data backed up? Your tracker data lives on your device. Reinstalling the app or switching devices doesn't automatically restore it.
  • Can I export my data? Not yet — if this matters to you, email me and let me know, it helps prioritize what to build next.
  • How do I request a refund? Refunds for App Store purchases are handled by Apple, not by me directly. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com.
