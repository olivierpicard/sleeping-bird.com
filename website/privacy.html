Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 12, 2026

ArperBird ("the app") is developed and maintained by Olivier Picard ("I", "me"). This policy explains what data the app collects, why, and how it's handled.

Your tracker data stays on your device

The metrics you create and the entries you log are stored locally on your device. I don't run a server that stores your tracker data, and there's no account or sign-in.

What's collected, and why

  • AI-generated suggestions. Text you type when creating a tracker is sent to Google's Firebase AI service to generate the tracker's shape (chart type, fields, name suggestions). This text may be processed by Google's infrastructure per their terms.
  • Anonymous usage analytics. The app uses PostHog (hosted in the EU) to understand feature usage and catch crashes. Events are tied to a random identifier generated on your device — not your name, email, or any account.
  • Purchases. Subscriptions are handled by Apple's StoreKit and mirrored to RevenueCat to manage entitlements. Payment details are handled entirely by Apple; I never see your card information.

What isn't collected

ArperBird doesn't require an account, doesn't collect your name or email unless you email support directly, doesn't show ads, and doesn't sell data to third parties.

Third-party services

  • Google Firebase (AI Logic) — AI-generated tracker suggestions
  • PostHog — anonymous analytics and crash reporting
  • RevenueCat / Apple StoreKit — subscription management

Each of these processes only the data described above, under their own privacy terms.

Data deletion

Deleting the app removes your locally stored tracker data. To request deletion of any analytics data tied to your device's anonymous identifier, contact me at the email below.

Children

ArperBird is not directed at children under 13, and I don't knowingly collect data from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.

Contact
